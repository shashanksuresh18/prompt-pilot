# PromptPilot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 February 2026_

PromptPilot is a Chrome extension that runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.

- **Scope**: The extension only runs on authorized AI platform domains:
  - `chatgpt.com` / `chat.openai.com` (ChatGPT)
  - `claude.ai` (Claude)
  - `gemini.google.com` (Gemini)
- **Data Processing**: It reads the text you type into the input area of these platforms ONLY when you click the "Enhance" button. It transforms this text into a structured prompt and displays a preview. It only modifies your input when you click "Use This" in the preview modal.
- **Local-Only**: All processing happens locally in your browser’s background service worker. The extension **does not send** your prompts or any other content to any external server or third party.
- **No Tracking**: The extension does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies.
- **Permissions**:
  - `storage`: Used only to save your local UI preferences (default Tone and enabled Categories). No chat content is stored.
  - `host_permissions`: Required to inject the "Enhance" button on supported AI platforms.
